    Join the module's WiFi
    
        
    Your ELRS or TBS module can make its own WiFi network. The Pi can join it and take the sticks from there, with no home network in between.
    
        
    
          
          
        
    
        
    Start the module's WiFi first (ELRS: the Lua script's WiFi Connectivity → Enable WiFi; it makes ExpressLRS TX, password expresslrs). The Pi then leaves this network: to see this page again, join the module's network with this phone too, or look at the address on the goggles' main menu.
    
      
    
    
    

    
    
    
      
    Video
    The picture: composite (AV) to a 5.8 GHz video transmitter, or HDMI.

    Settings
    
        
    
        
      
          
    • after reboot AV ↔ HDMI and the TV standard: the Pi sets up its video output when it starts. Save, then Reboot now (about 30 s; it may restart once more by itself to switch the output).
      • 
          
    • restarts the game Shape, composite lines, progressive detail, HDMI mode, border and text size: the game restarts in a second or two.
      • 
          
    • at once The rest applies while you fly.
      • 
        
    
        
    Wiring for AV: the 3.5 mm jack's sleeve (the last ring) is video, the ring before it ground. A TV's yellow input works too. If nothing shows after a change, plug in HDMI, or edit sticktime.txt on the SD card from a PC.
    
      
    
      
    
        
    VTX control
    –
    
        
    Set the video transmitter's channel, power and pit mode from here instead of its button, over its SmartAudio or Tramp pad: one wire from pin 7 through a 1 kΩ resistor (the user guide shows how). Measure the pad first: the Pi's pins take 3.3 V only.

    Networks
    
        
    Scan lists the networks the Pi can see (the WiFi joystick pauses for a moment while it scans). 2.4 GHz is best: 5 GHz WiFi can disturb 5.8 GHz video, and the ELRS module is 2.4 GHz only.
    
        
      
        
        
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Hotspot

            About
            
        
            StickTime Pi runs StickTime, the FPV simulator for EdgeTX radios, on a Raspberry Pi, with the sticks of your own radio and the picture in your goggles. By juricabi. GPL-2.0.
            
        
            Inside: Lua 5.3 (MIT), stb (public domain), Roboto (OFL). The track editor is StickTime's own page; the WiFi joystick follows ELRS-Crossfire-WiFi-Joystick.
