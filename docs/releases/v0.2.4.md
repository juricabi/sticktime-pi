## What's new

- **Ready for StickTime 1.9.0 and its Colors** (this image ships it). On the AV output to your goggles:
  - the game's **Settings → Colors** is **Auto**, which is the natural **Summer** look there (Classic on
    HDMI). Summer, Evening and Overcast have a sky that fades into the haze at the horizon, duller grass and
    calmer colors: easier on the eyes, and clean on analog video.
  - the text (lap, time, speed...) has a black outline, like Betaflight's OSD, readable over sky and grass.
- **Smoother motion at 60 Hz**: the Pi gives the game each frame's exact display time. Until now the game
  only had its 10 ms clock, so at NTSC's 60 Hz each step of the simulation was 0.6 ms off on average, at times
  3 ms: a slight stutter in fast turns. (PAL's 50 Hz was already even.)
- User guide: **Easy on the eyes in goggles**: Colors, `av_mode = progressive` (no flicker of thin lines and
  text), NTSC or PAL, and `ntsc` vs `ntsc-j` when blacks look grey.

## Update from the web page

From 0.2.1 or newer: Updates → Check → install StickTime Pi 0.2.4, then Check again to install StickTime
1.9.0. If you copied a `StickTime.lua` of your own onto the card (a test build), automatic updates leave it
as it is: install the game once from the Updates page (it keeps a copy of yours in `sticktime/backup/`).

From 0.1.0 or 0.2.0: those updaters can't install updates (see the 0.2.1 notes): flash this image once.
Copy `sticktime.txt`, `sticktime/music` and, from `sticktime/sd/SCRIPTS/TOOLS`, `StickTime.dat`,
`StickTimeBW.dat`, `StickTimeLite/data.txt`, `StickTimeTracks` and `StickTimeGhosts` to your PC first, and
put them back after flashing (the WiFi password isn't in `sticktime.txt` any more after a boot: type it
again).
