## What's new

- **Latency says what it measures.** The Status page's latency tile reads "stick to video out": the figure
  ends where the picture leaves the Pi (the VTX or the HDMI port). Analog goggles add next to nothing on top,
  a digital screen or HDMI goggles add their own 10-30 ms; the user guide ("Latency and frame times") says so.
- README: the printed case in the pictures (the case with the monitor and the radio, the open case in the
  wiring section) and today's VTX control page with a Tramp VTX found on one pin.
- Everything from 0.2.7: VTX control on one pin working in the running game.
